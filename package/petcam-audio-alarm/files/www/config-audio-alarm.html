Audio Alarm

Watches the camera's own mic for a sustained loud sound (e.g. a crying baby) above the room's rolling ambient level, and sends an alert with photo/video when it fires.

Detection

Sound above the activity ratio accumulates over time (and decays when it drops back down); once the accumulated total passes the integral threshold, it triggers. Catches sustained or repeated moderate noise (e.g. continuous talking) without needing a single very loud instant. Raise either value to make this less sensitive to a busy room.

While the sound keeps recurring (e.g. continued crying), the recording keeps extending by this amount instead of cutting off.

Available Services

Photo/video capability per service is configured on each service's own page (same services used by Motion Detection).
